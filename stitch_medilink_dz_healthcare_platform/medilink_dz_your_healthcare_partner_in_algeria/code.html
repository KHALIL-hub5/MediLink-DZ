Frequently Asked Questions

Is MediLink free for patients? expand_more
Yes, searching for doctors, viewing pharmacy lists, and booking appointments are completely free for all patients.
How do I verify a doctor's credentials? expand_more
Every medical practitioner on our platform undergoes a rigorous verification process, including proof of registration with the Algerian National Council of Medical Ethics.
Is my medical data secure? expand_more
We use end-to-end encryption for all medical records and messages, adhering to international healthcare data protection standards and Algerian privacy regulations.
