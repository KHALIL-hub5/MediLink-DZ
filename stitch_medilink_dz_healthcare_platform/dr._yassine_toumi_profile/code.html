person_search Biography

Dr. Amine Rahmani is a distinguished cardiologist based in Algiers, specializing in interventional cardiology and cardiovascular disease management. With over 15 years of experience, he has pioneered several minimally invasive cardiac procedures at the University Hospital Center (CHU Mustapha Pacha).

He is dedicated to providing compassionate, evidence-based care, focusing on preventive measures and advanced treatment options for heart failure and coronary artery disease. His patient-centric approach ensures that each individual receives a tailored treatment plan optimized for their lifestyle.
